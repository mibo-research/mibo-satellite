# MIBO-Education Satellite Charter v1.0

## 1. Name

MIBO-Education.

## 2. Distinct observation object

Educational behavior expressed by general-purpose AI systems under synchronized, independently replicated observations.

## 3. Scientific purpose

AI capability benchmarks measure what a model can do. MIBO-Education measures what kind of educator a model becomes.

## 4. Relationship to MIBO Core

Education inherits permanent service-lineage identity, Wave conventions, independent replication, distributional observation, hashing, immutable raw records, re-observability, correction history, and prospective amendment. Education data are not automatically pooled with Core data.

## 5. Observation design

The primary planned condition is Frozen Battery × CLOSED environment × permanent model/service series × independent k replications × synchronized Wave. W0 is engineering-only. W01 is the first planned longitudinal baseline and remains blocked until every required operational lock and human authorization is recorded.

## 7. Outcomes and codebook
