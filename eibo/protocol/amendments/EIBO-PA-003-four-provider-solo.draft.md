## Coding and masking limitations

Code every rateable primary observation using the exact referenced anchors;
technical failures receive no educational score. Preserve refusals, blocks,
empty completed responses and truncation as outcomes, not technical retries.
The blind coding view suppresses model/provider/Wave metadata where feasible;
visible answers must not be edited to conceal self-identification or style.
The sole operator may recognize identities or remember execution information.
Therefore this is masked coding materials, not a guarantee of perfect blinding,
independent coding, or a second rater. Record unmasking incidents and limitations.

Coding-QA method, sample, selection seed, delay before any repeat coding, and
decision rules remain pending. No agreement statistic or validated reliability
is claimed. Any repeat-coding procedure would measure intra-coder consistency
only and cannot establish independent-rater agreement or correctness.

## Volume, synchronization and authorization remain open

The inherited proposal is 70 items × four series × k=10 = 2,800 primary
observations (2,100 CLOSED and 700 NATIVE). This is NOT an approved sample size,
cost budget, or coding-workload commitment. No additional no-search arm is proposed.

At the historical Google 65-second generation-start gap, 700 starts require at
least 699 × 65 = 45,435 seconds (12h 37m 15s), excluding the final response and
other delays. A 12-hour completion target is infeasible under that pacing. A new
time window, replication count or empirically justified pacing needs an explicit
prospective decision; do not change legacy W01 windows. A 24-hour bound is not
guaranteed feasible merely because it exceeds this lower bound.

financial/request/attempt ceilings, retry waits, exact models, provider profiles,
