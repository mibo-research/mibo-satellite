# EIBO Charter — engineering draft

## 1. Name

EIBO. The historical project name `MIBO-Education` remains embedded in the byte-frozen v1.0 scientific record.

## 2. Distinct observation object

Educational behavior expressed by general-purpose AI systems under synchronized, independently replicated observations.

## 3. Scientific purpose

AI capability benchmarks measure what a model can do. EIBO measures what kind of educator a model becomes.

## 4. Relationship to MIBO Core

Education inherits permanent service-lineage identity, Wave conventions, independent replication, distributional observation, hashing, immutable raw records, re-observability, correction history, and prospective amendment. Education data are not automatically pooled with Core data.

## 5. Observation design

The primary planned condition is Frozen Battery × CLOSED environment × permanent model/service series × independent k replications × synchronized Wave. W0 is engineering-only. W01 is the first planned longitudinal baseline and is blocked until all source artifacts and approvals exist.

## 6. Instrument or stimulus

EBB-JA v1.0, exactly 70 approved Frozen Items. Its authoritative text and external per-prompt SHA-256 lock are imported unchanged from the v1.0 scientific source bundle.

## 7. Outcomes and codebook

The historical scientific record is governed by the frozen MIBO-Education Codebook v1.0 and Item-Level Scoring Manual v1.0. Prospective EIBO Waves additionally require an approved successor protocol or amendment; no replacement anchors are inferred here.

## 8. Missingness, deviations, and attrition

Technical failures, retries, missing observations, exact-model availability, returned-model mismatches, and protocol deviations remain visible. Valid refusals and undesirable outputs are observations, not missingness.

## 9. Ethics, law, terms, and data custody

Live qualification and any future official Wave require recorded protocol-owner, terms, and ethics/governance determinations. One named human may operate the system, but automation and agents cannot approve these records. L0 may contain restricted content and is not committed to this public repository.

## 10. Operating and coding model

EIBO uses one accountable human operator with automated execution, preservation, QC, and export. Primary coding uses one human coder. No two-human inter-rater reliability claim is made. Optional AI secondary coding is permitted only as a separately preserved sensitivity analysis and is not a substitute for a human coder.

## 11. Openness, corrections, and withdrawals

L0 is immutable. Corrections create versioned derived records. Protocol amendments apply prospectively and historical artifacts remain inspectable.

## 12. Lifecycle status

Incubation / engineering implementation. It is not yet a Registered or Active Satellite.

## 13. Approval and version

Engineering draft 0.1. Scientific approval pending. See `BLOCKERS.md`.
